Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

1. How images are processed

Every image editing feature on PixelCraft Studio (such as resizing) runs directly in your web browser using JavaScript. Images you upload are never sent to or stored on any server — the edited result is downloaded straight to your device.

2. Information we collect

This site has no sign-up or account system, and we do not separately collect personally identifying information such as your name or email address. That said, our advertising and analytics providers may automatically collect certain information, as described in section 3.

3. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may use third-party advertising services, including Google, which may use cookies. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or disable cookies entirely in your browser settings.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to collect anonymized statistics such as visitor counts and pageviews to help improve the service. This information is not used to identify individuals.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your images or personal information with third parties. Advertising and analytics vendors mentioned above may process data under their own respective policies.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the About page.

7. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in law or in our service. Any changes will be posted on this page.
