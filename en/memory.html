How to play

16 cards (8 pairs) start face down. Flip two at a time - if they match, that pair stays face up; if not, both flip back down. Match all 8 pairs to finish, and your move count and time are tracked along the way.

Tips

  • Remember the position of cards you've already seen to cut down your move count.
  • Working through the board edge by edge makes positions easier to keep track of.
  • If you're going for a low move count, avoid guessing unless you're fairly sure of a match.
