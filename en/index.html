About PixelCraft Studio

PixelCraft Studio is a free support site for people making games. Right in your browser, you can resize images, grab royalty-free pixel icons to download, or check out a summary of game-making tools that are actually widely used. Topics that trip people up in practice, like sprite sizing or free asset licensing, are covered in our guides. We plan to keep adding more features and resources for game development.

How it works

  • 1. UploadSelect or drag and drop the image you want to resize.
  • 2. Set sizeType a width/height or use a quick-scale button.
  • 3. DownloadCheck the preview, then save the resized image.

Why PixelCraft Studio?

Since your image is processed locally in the browser and never uploaded to a server, you can safely resize personal photos too. There's nothing to install and no account required, and you keep full control over the output size while preserving image quality.
